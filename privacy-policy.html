VevahaMatrimonial Services ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through vevahamatrimony.com, why we collect it, how we use, share and protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It is published under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules (the "DPDP Act"). For this policy, we act as the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you give us

Account details: name, gender, date of birth, mobile number, alternate mobile number (optional, for example a parent's or guardian's number), email, password, and whether the profile is created for yourself, a son, daughter, sibling or other relative.

Profile details: religion, caste/community, mother tongue, marital status, height, education, occupation, income, location, family details, lifestyle, interests, partner preferences and "about me" text.

Photographs: profile and gallery photos you upload.

Horoscope details: date, time and place of birth, star/rashi, gotra, manglik status and horoscope files, and anything you type into the Astro AI chatbot.

Identity verification data: government ID (such as Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence or voter ID), ID number (partially masked where possible), the name, date of birth and photo on it, and a selfie or liveness check if requested.

Payment information: plan purchased, amount, date, transaction ID and payment mode. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay; we do not store full card numbers, CVV or UPI PIN.

Communications: chat messages, interests sent and received, shortlists, blocks, reports, details shared on voice or video calls features, and your emails or calls to our support team.

b) Information collected automatically

Device and browser type, operating system, IP address, language, time zone, and approximate location from your IP.

Pages viewed, search filters used, profiles viewed, time spent, referral source, crash logs and activity logs.

Cookies and similar technologies (see section 7).

c) Information from others

From a parent, guardian or relative who creates or manages a profile for you.

From our representatives, where a personal-visit verification or personal matchmaking service is provided (for example, confirmation of address, family and background details).

From ID verification and payment partners, who confirm results such as "verified" or "payment successful".

From other members, such as reports or complaints about you.

2. Sensitive Personal Data

The following are treated as sensitive personal data or information: passwords, financial and payment details, health or physical condition you choose to disclose, government ID numbers, sexual orientation if disclosed, and biometric data such as a selfie or face match used for verification. Religion, caste, horoscope and family details are highly personal. We collect these only with your explicit consent, only because they are needed for matchmaking, and you may choose not to provide them (although some features or verification may then not be available).

3. Why We Use Your Information

We process your data only for specified, lawful purposes:

  • to create and manage your account and show your profile to suitable members;
  • to suggest matches and provide search, interests, shortlists, chat and calling features, and personal matchmaking for plans that include it;
  • to verify your identity and prevent fake profiles, fraud, impersonation and misuse;
  • to process payments, issue GST invoices and manage memberships, auto-renewals and refunds;
  • to provide horoscope-related responses through Astro AI;
  • to send service messages (OTP, alerts, match and message notifications, payment receipts) by SMS, email, WhatsApp, push notification or call;
  • to send offers and updates, only if you have opted in (you can opt out anytime);
  • to provide customer support, handle complaints and enforce our Terms;
  • to analyse and improve the Platform, fix errors and ensure security; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from courts, police and government authorities.

4. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your free, specific, informed and unambiguous consent given when you register, upload data or tick the relevant boxes, and for certain legitimate uses permitted by law (for example, legal compliance and security). You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us or using your settings; this may mean we can no longer provide some or all services, and withdrawal does not affect processing done earlier. If a parent or guardian creates a profile for you, they confirm they have your consent. If you add an alternate mobile number belonging to someone else, you confirm that person has agreed to its use and display as described in this policy.

5. Who Can See Your Information

Other members: Your profile details, and photos and horoscope as per your privacy settings, are visible to registered members. Your primary and alternate mobile numbers are shown only to paid members, and only when they use a contact view available under their plan; free members cannot see them. Your email is shown only to members you choose. Government ID details, income documents and payment data are never shown to other members.

Service providers: We share limited data with trusted vendors who work for us under confidentiality terms, such as cloud hosting, ID verification partners, Razorpay (payments), SMS/email/WhatsApp providers, analytics and customer support tools, and any provider that powers Astro AI.

Legal and safety: We may disclose data to government agencies, law enforcement or courts when legally required, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our members, the public or us.

Business transfer: If we merge or sell our business, your data may be transferred to the successor, which must honour this policy.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

6. Photos, Horoscopes and Chats

You decide the visibility of your photos and horoscope (for example, visible to all, only to members you accept, or only to premium members). Please remember that any member who can see a photo may take a screenshot, and we cannot fully prevent that. Chat messages are accessible to the sender and the recipient; we access them only as described in the Terms.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, protect against fraud, and understand how the Platform is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly. Where we use analytics or advertising tools (such as Google Analytics), they may collect data under their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention

Profile data is kept while your account is active.

If you delete your profile, we remove or anonymise your data within 30 days, except what we must keep by law, such as payment and GST invoice records (up to 8 years under tax laws), and records needed for fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal claims.

Accounts inactive for 24 months may be deactivated after notice.

ID verification documents are kept only as long as needed for verification and legal compliance, then securely deleted or masked.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable security practices, including HTTPS/TLS encryption, encrypted storage of sensitive data, access controls, password hashing, regular security reviews and staff confidentiality obligations, consistent with Indian law. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password, never share your OTP, and log out on shared devices. If there is a personal data breach, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

10. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access a summary of your personal data and know who it has been shared with;
  • correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data (most can be edited in your profile);
  • erase your data when it is no longer needed or when you withdraw consent;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • grievance redressal, and to approach the Data Protection Board of India if unsatisfied; and
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of your death or incapacity.

To exercise these rights, email vevahamatrimonydb@gmail.com or call +91 94871 61672. We may ask you to verify your identity first, and will respond within a reasonable time, not later than the period prescribed by law.

11. Children

The Platform is only for adults who are of legal marriageable age. We do not knowingly collect data of anyone under 18. If we find that a minor's profile exists, we will delete it immediately. Please report any such profile to us.

12. Transfers Outside India

Your data is primarily stored on servers in India. If any of our service providers process data outside India, we do so only where permitted by Indian law and with appropriate safeguards. Members who are located abroad (NRIs) can view profiles from outside India.

13. Third-Party Links

The Platform may contain links to other sites and social media pages. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and urge you to read their policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version with the updated date and, for material changes, notify you by email, SMS or on the Platform and seek fresh consent where required.
