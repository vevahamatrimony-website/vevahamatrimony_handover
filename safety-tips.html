Profile Safety Guidelines

Do's

  • Use recent, clear, and genuine photographs
  • Provide accurate information about yourself
  • Be honest about your background and expectations
  • Use the optional document verification feature
  • Keep your profile information updated
  • Choose a strong, unique password

Don'ts

  • Never use someone else's photographs
  • Don't provide false information
  • Avoid sharing your exact home address
  • Don't include sensitive personal information
  • Never share your login credentials
  • Don't create multiple profiles

Communication Safety

Safe Communication Practices

  • Always start with platform's messaging system
  • Take time to know the person through conversations
  • Ask relevant questions about background
  • Be cautious of pressure for immediate meetings

Red Flags in Communication

  • Requests for money or financial assistance
  • Pressure to move off platform immediately
  • Reluctance to share basic information
  • Inconsistent stories or information

Meeting in Person - Safety Protocol

Choose Public Places

Always meet in crowded, public locations like cafes, restaurants, or malls

Daytime Meetings

Prefer daylight hours for first meetings and keep them brief (1-2 hours)

Inform Someone

Tell a trusted person about your meeting plans and arrange your own transportation

Financial Safety

Golden Rules

  • NEVER send money to anyone you meet through the platform
  • NO financial transactions with potential matches
  • BEWARE of sob stories or emergency money requests
  • DON'T share financial information like bank details
