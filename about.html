About vevahamatrimony

Dedicated to bringing families together through meaningful connections and lasting love stories

Our Story

vevahamatrimony was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves to find their perfect life partner. Founded in 2024, we have been dedicated to connecting hearts and creating lasting relationships through our innovative matchmaking platform.

With deep roots in Tamil culture and traditions, we understand the importance of family values, compatibility, and shared dreams. Our platform combines traditional matchmaking wisdom with modern technology to help you find your ideal match.

Today, we are proud to serve thousands of families across Tamil Nadu and beyond, helping them take the next step in their life's journey through meaningful connections.

Our Mission

To provide a secure, trusted, and comprehensive platform that facilitates meaningful connections between individuals seeking marriage, while preserving the essence of Indian cultural values and traditions.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted matrimonial platform, known for creating successful, happy marriages that strengthen families and communities across the nation.
